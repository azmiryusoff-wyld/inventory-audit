How it works (five steps each month)
  1. Start the month for an outlet below, or start every outlet at once on Month close. Opening stock is copied from last month's closing count.
  2. Upload the files on the audit's Dashboard: the POS item sales report (chef), the Xero supplier invoices and the closing stock count (procurement). Each upload has an empty template to download.
  3. Fix the data checks listed under the files. Each one says who fixes it and what it leaves out of the numbers. One wrong line can be fixed on Uploaded data without re-uploading.
  4. Read the result. Variance is actual minus theoretical cost; within tolerance shows green. Variance & inventory shows which items drive it.
  5. Sign off (CEO). This freezes the month's numbers.

Words like COGS, yield and the menu classes are explained on Help; dotted words show an explanation when you point at them.
